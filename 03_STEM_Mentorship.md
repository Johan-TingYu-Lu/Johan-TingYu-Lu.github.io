My focus is translating real-world R&D methodologies (Agile, FMEA, CAD, Coding) into hands-on learning, successfully guiding **over 200 students into top-tier university programs** (including 16 medical/dental admissions).

The resulting `179 Cidal` Risk Management Plan was officially praised by expert competition reviewers as "displaying an engineering maturity far exceeding high school levels" and representing a "practically applicable industry standard."
