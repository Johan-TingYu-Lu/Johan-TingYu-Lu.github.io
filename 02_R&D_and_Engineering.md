# ⚙️ Offshore Wind & Prototyping Engineering

## ⚡ Offshore Wind: Maritime Seafastening FEA (SOIC)
During my tenure at the national Ship and Ocean Industries R&D Center (SOIC), I spearheaded the structural analysis for offshore wind turbine installation logistics.
*   **Challenge**: Ensuring massive offshore turbine components remain secure on transport vessels under extreme North Sea/Taiwan Strait weather conditions.
*   **Methodology**: Utilized DNV/GL maritime standards to calculate dynamic sea loads. Applied extensive FEA using **MSC/NASTRAN**, **ABAQUS**, and **3D BEAM**.
*   **Impact**: Published fundamental guidelines at the 28th Conference on Ship and Ocean Engineering (2016), setting a standard for local offshore supply chains.
