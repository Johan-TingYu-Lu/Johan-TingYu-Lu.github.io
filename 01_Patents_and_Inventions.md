# 💡 Patents & Fluid Systems Innovations

As a lead engineer and inventor, my work spans three distinct industries, culminating in **5 national patents**.

## 🌊 Sustainable Cleantech & Water Treatment
Between 2024 and 2025, I focus on the high-value aquaculture and "fish-electricity symbiosis" sectors, leading to three critical patents aimed at improving oxygen mass transfer and filtration efficiency.

1. **Aeration Tube & Device (Invention Patent I909845 - 2025)**
    * Designed an innovative flow mechanics tube to maximize dissolved oxygen generation for large-scale aquaculture.
2. **Filtration Device (Utility Model M676719 - 2025)**
    * Developed an integrated filtration pipeline capable of reducing bio-waste buildup autonomously.

## 🚙 Amphibious Vehicle Retractable Suspension
Working at the national Ship and Ocean Industries R&D Center (SOIC):
* **Retractable Suspension System (Invention I592319 - 2017)**
    * Engineered a space-saving, highly durable retractable suspension block for a new amphibious ATV. The mechanism prevents underwater drag while supporting terrestrial load requirements. Over $1.2M in government R&D funding was secured, leading to successful tech transfer MOUs with ATV manufacturers.
